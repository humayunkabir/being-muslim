# Being Muslim — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 22 October 2026

Being Muslim is an Android app for prayer times, the qibla, the Hijri date,
reminders, and tasbih. This page explains what the app does with your
information. It applies to the Android app published on Google Play under the
package name `app.beingmuslim.android`.

**Contact:** [humayunkabir.cep@gmail.com](mailto:humayunkabir.cep@gmail.com)

## The short version

- The app works without an account and without a network connection.
- Your city, settings, reminders, and tasbih stay on your device.
- An optional account stores only your sign-in identity, your settings, and your
  tasbih total, so they can follow you to another device.
- A problem report is sent only when you tap **Send report**.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling of data.

## What stays on your device

These are stored by the app on your phone, and without an account they are not
sent to us:

- the city you choose;
- your calculation settings, offsets, high-latitude rule, and Hijri day shift;
- your reminders and quiet hours;
- your tasbih label, target, and count;
- the prayer times, qibla bearing, and Hijri date calculated from them.

If you sign in, only your settings and tasbih total (the current count) are
copied to the account (see below). The city, reminders, the tasbih label and
target, and every calculated result stay on the phone.

Devotional data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device
transfer. Your chosen city and calculation settings are ordinary app settings,
so Android's own backup may include them if you have backup enabled; that copy
is controlled by your Google account, not by us. Uninstalling the app or
clearing its data deletes the local copy.

### Location

With your permission, the app reads your **approximate location once** to
suggest the nearest city in its bundled catalog. The location is used on the
device for that suggestion and is not stored or sent anywhere. You can deny the
permission and pick a city yourself; every feature still works.

### Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local prayer reminders on your device.
Reminder content is generated on the phone. No notification data is sent to us.

## The optional account

You can sign in with Google or Apple to carry your settings and tasbih total to
another device. Signing in is never required.

When you create an account, the app stores on its self-hosted Appwrite service:

- the user id Appwrite assigns;
- the name and email address your Google or Apple sign-in shares with the app;
- your settings: calculation method, Asr juristic method, high-latitude rule,
  Hijri day shift, per-prayer offsets, and language;
- your tasbih total;
- when the synced row was created and last updated — the service's own row
  metadata, not a record of when you prayed.

It does **not** store your location, calculated prayer times, the Hijri date,
qibla direction, reminder choices, tasbih label or target, or any record of when
you prayed or used a devotional feature.

The account data is kept while the account exists. Signing out leaves it in
place so it returns if you sign in again. It is sent over HTTPS, and the app
keeps its session token in Android Keystore-backed storage.

Google or Apple handles your sign-in under its own privacy policy. The app
receives only the name and email address you agree to share.

## Problem reports

If a calculated time looks wrong, you can send a problem report. The screen
lists everything attached before you send it:

- the city you selected and its coordinates;
- the time zone and the date;
- the calculation method and its parameters, the Asr juristic method, the
  high-latitude rule, your Hijri day shift, and your prayer-time offsets;
- the calculated times shown on the times screen, before your own adjustments;
- the app version and the calculation library version;
- anything you type in the note.

The report is used only to check the calculation and fix a wrong time. It is not
linked to your account, and it carries no device identifier, location history,
or email address unless you put personal details in the note yourself. The
operator deletes a report once the problem is resolved; there is no automatic
expiry, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time. Please do not include
personal details in the note.

## What we never do

- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
- No selling or renting of personal data.
- No access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone, or precise location.
- No background location.

## Your choices and rights

- **Use the app without an account.** Every feature works signed out.
- **Delete your account.** Open **Account → Delete account**, or email us; see
  [Delete your account](#delete-your-account).
- **Export your account data in the app:** open **Account → Export my data** to
  save a JSON file through the system file picker.
- **Request deletion of a problem report,** or ask a privacy question, by
  emailing [humayunkabir.cep@gmail.com](mailto:humayunkabir.cep@gmail.com). For a
  report, include the approximate date and the city, because reports are not
  linked to an account.

## Delete your account

In the app, open **Account → Delete account** and confirm. This removes the
synced settings and tasbih total and the sign-in identity from the service. The
account cannot be signed into again, and a later sign-in with the same Google or
Apple account starts a new, empty account. Your device's data stays on the
device.

You can also request deletion by emailing
[humayunkabir.cep@gmail.com](mailto:humayunkabir.cep@gmail.com) from the address
you signed in with. We respond within 30 days. Deleting a problem report is a
separate request, because reports are not linked to an account.

## Security

Data sent to the account service is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. The service
is operated for this app, and access to it is restricted to the operator.
No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app collects
as little as the features need.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created
an account, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A material change
will be described in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
